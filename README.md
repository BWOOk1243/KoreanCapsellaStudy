KoreanCapsellaStudy

> This repository is for R scripts and raw data for the statistical analyses employed in the study entitled "Local Genetic Signatures of a Global Weed: Population Structure and Ecological Drivers of Capsella bursa-pastoris diversity in the Korean Peninsula" published through Ecology and Evolution.
 
> Byungwook Choi*, Eunsuk Kim, Peter Tiffin, and Sang-Tae Kim*
> *Co-corresponding authors (bwook1243@gmail.com)
> Keywords: Population differentiation, Admixture, Gene flow, RAD-seq
